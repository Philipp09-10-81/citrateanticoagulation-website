Standard modality

CVVHD with regional citrate anticoagulation

Core principle, typical target values, and workflow outline — as a starting point for your center-specific protocol.

Principle

Citrate is infused pre-dilutionally into the extracorporeal circuit and chelates ionized calcium, inhibiting the coagulation cascade within the filter. Calcium is separately replaced systemically to maintain physiological calcium levels in the patient.
